MyGiftCards

Which gift card works here?

You are at the till with four gift cards from work. One of them is accepted here — but which? By the time you have checked three websites, the queue has grown and you have paid full price with your own card. Again.

MyGiftCards answers that in about two seconds. Type part of the shop's name and it shows which of your cards is accepted there, how much is left, and when it expires — naming the merchant that matched, so you can see it understood you.

What it does

  • Searches merchant lists covering BuyMe (All, Chef, Style, Together and more), All-inZone, SuperZone, GiftZone, ChefZone, SpaZone and LOVE.
  • Hebrew and English, with forgiving matching: partial words, final-letter folding, niqqud ignored, and wrong-keyboard-layout typing.
  • Sorts cards soonest-to-expire first, because the money evaporates.
  • Card numbers stay on the device, encrypted with an Android Keystore key.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Privacy

Your cards are stored on your phone and are not uploaded anywhere. The full policy is here: MyGiftCards privacy policy.

What this site serves

This is the app's data source, not a marketing site. It publishes the merchant lists that every install reads, refreshed weekly by a GitHub Actions workflow. Phones fetch the small manifest first and re-download a list only when its hash has changed.

FileWhat it is
manifest.json sha256 and size of every published file — the thing phones poll
catalog.json Card types, aliases, and where each list is fetched from
stores/<card-type>.json One merchant list per card type
